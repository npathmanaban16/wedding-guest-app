Everything, in one place

The spreadsheet, the group text, the PDF. Replaced.

Tetherly gathers everything scattered across your wedding week into a single elegant app your guests will open again and again.

Live schedule

Every event, every venue, every dress code, with "happening now" highlights and last-minute updates pushed to guests instantly.

Announcements

Guest details

Arrival times, hotel plans, dietary restrictions. Guests fill it in once, and you see it all in one place. No more email forms or reply-all threads.

Song requests

Meet Ask · AI assistant

The AI that answers your guests, so you don't have to.

Ask is the floating assistant in every corner of Tetherly. Guests tap it, ask a question, and get a warm, immediate answer, grounded entirely in your wedding, before they'd otherwise text you at 11 p.m.

  • Grounded in your wedding. Pulls from your schedule, dress codes, packing list, and travel guide. No hallucinated hotels, planners, or times.
  • Personalized per guest. Hides events and items the guest isn't invited to. Bridal-party details never leak to the general list.
  • Contextual to each tab. On Schedule, starters shift to "What should I wear?" On the Guide tab, "Best half-day activity near the hotel?"
  • Insights for you. Every question is logged so you can see what guests are curious about, and update your content once.

Founder story

I built Tetherly for our wedding
One place for every guest.

While planning our destination wedding in Switzerland, we realized how difficult it was to gather information from more than 100 guests while also keeping everyone informed, prepared, and connected throughout a multi-day celebration.

Important details were scattered across our wedding website, emails, group chats, and individual messages. We wanted one place where guests could easily find the weekend itinerary, dress codes, travel guidance, packing reminders, announcements, and everything else they needed without having to search for it.

But we wanted Tetherly to be more than a place for logistics. We also wanted to build excitement before the wedding and help our guests get to know one another before they arrived.

So I built Tetherly.

During our wedding weekend, our guests used the app to follow event schedules, receive real-time updates, request songs, connect with one another, and even get personalized local recommendations. It also helped our planning team coordinate guest arrivals and deliver welcome gifts to guests' hotel rooms more seamlessly.

Seeing how much easier and more connected the experience felt showed us that Tetherly could help other couples too.

Tetherly was created so couples can help every guest feel part of the celebration before they arrive, and connected to every moment once it begins.
