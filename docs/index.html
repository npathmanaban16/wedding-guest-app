Everything, in one place

The spreadsheet, the group text, the PDF — replaced.

Tetherly gathers everything scattered across your wedding week into a single elegant app your guests will open — again and again.

Live schedule

Every event, every venue, every dress code — with "happening now" highlights and last-minute updates pushed to guests instantly.

Guest photo album

A shared, private album for every guest to upload the shots they took — everything, not just what makes it to Instagram.

Song requests

Guests suggest the tracks they want to hear. Your DJ gets a ranked list, not a hundred forgotten group-text messages.

Travel guide

Curated hotels, restaurants, and things to do at the destination — with maps and notes from you, not the internet.

Packing list

Weather-aware, per-event packing lists guests can check off. No more last-minute "wait, is it black tie?" texts at midnight.

Announcements

Send a push to every guest — "shuttle in 15", "wear white for the mehndi" — and know it landed on the right screens.

Guest groups

Wedding party, family, plus-ones, kids — show the right events to the right people without a spreadsheet in sight.

Accommodations

Room blocks, booking codes, transport windows, check-in details — bundled with the guests who need each one.

Live vendor queues

Henna, photos, mehndi stations — guests join the line from their phone and know where they are in real time.

Founder story

We built Tetherly for our own wedding. Everyone kept asking for it.

Our guests were flying in from three continents for four days of events. The Google Doc kept getting stale. The group texts scattered the schedule across six different chats. Someone asked what to wear to the mehndi at 2 a.m. We thought: this can be better — and beautiful.

So we built an app just for our wedding. A single elegant place with the schedule, the packing list, the songs, the photos, the travel notes for a city half our guests had never been to. We shipped it two weeks before the wedding.

By day two, guests were opening it more than Instagram. Aunts who "don't do apps" were requesting songs. Our henna artist asked if she could use the queue for another wedding next month. A planner friend asked when it would be available for her couples.

Tetherly is that app, rebuilt for every couple who wants their wedding to feel like the good part of a wedding — the people, the moments — without the group-text tax on the way there.
